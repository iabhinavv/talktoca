Company & LLP Registration

Private Limited, LLP, OPC and partnership registration in Chandigarh, filed end to end.

Registering the business is the first decision with lasting tax consequences, and the one most often made on the basis of what a friend did. We take founders through the structure that actually fits their plans, then handle the filing end to end — name approval, incorporation, PAN, TAN, bank account and the post-incorporation compliances that catch new companies out.

Choosing between Private Limited, LLP, OPC and proprietorship

There is no universally correct answer. The right structure depends on who is putting money in, what liability you are exposed to, whether you intend to raise external capital, and how much annual compliance you are willing to carry.

Comparing the common Indian business structures
StructureBest suited toLiabilityAnnual compliance load
ProprietorshipSingle owner, low risk, testing an ideaUnlimited — personal assets exposedLightest: income tax return, GST if registered
Partnership firmTwo or more owners, traditional tradeUnlimited and jointLight: firm return, GST, partnership deed upkeep
LLPProfessional services, family businesses, JVsLimited to contributionModerate: LLP Form 8 and Form 11, income tax return
One Person CompanySolo founder wanting corporate statusLimitedModerate: ROC filings, audit, nominee requirement
Private LimitedStartups raising capital, scaling businessesLimitedHighest: statutory audit, AOC-4, MGT-7, board and general meetings

Two practical points founders underestimate. First, a private limited company must have its accounts audited every year regardless of turnover — that cost is permanent from day one. Second, converting later is not free: a proprietorship becoming a company means new registrations, transfer of assets and potential tax on that transfer. It is worth an hour of thought now.

What company registration involves

Incorporation runs through the MCA portal on the SPICe+ form, which bundles several approvals into one application:

  • Digital Signature Certificates for each proposed director and subscriber.
  • SPICe+ Part A — name reservation. Two names can be proposed; we check them against existing companies and registered trademarks first, because a name that clashes with a live trademark gets rejected and costs you a resubmission.
  • SPICe+ Part B — the incorporation application itself, allotting DIN to directors who do not already hold one.
  • e-MoA (INC-33) and e-AoA (INC-34) — the memorandum and articles, with the objects clause drafted to cover what you actually intend to do, plus room to grow.
  • AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35) — linked application covering GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, professional tax where applicable, and bank account opening.
  • PAN and TAN, issued along with the certificate of incorporation.

Documents you will need

  • PAN and Aadhaar of every proposed director and subscriber
  • Passport for any foreign national director, notarised or apostilled as required
  • Recent bank statement, electricity bill or mobile bill as address proof for each director
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Proof of the registered office — a recent utility bill plus the owner's no-objection certificate, or the rent agreement where premises are leased

Typical timeline

With complete documents, name approval usually comes through in one to three working days and incorporation follows within roughly a week, subject to the Registrar's queries and current processing volumes. The commonest cause of delay is not the department — it is address proof that does not match, or a proposed name that was never checked against the trademark register.

Do not miss INC-20A. A company with share capital must file the declaration of commencement of business within 180 days of incorporation, after the subscribers have actually paid in their subscription money. Missing it carries penalties and can lead to the company being struck off. It is the single most common first-year failure we are asked to fix.

After incorporation: what year one actually requires

  • Opening the bank account and bringing in subscription capital, then filing INC-20A
  • Appointing the first auditor within 30 days of incorporation and filing Form ADT-1
  • Issuing share certificates and paying stamp duty on them
  • Maintaining statutory registers and minutes of board meetings from the first meeting onward
  • GST registration where you cross the threshold or supply inter-state
  • The first annual filings — AOC-4 and MGT-7 or MGT-7A — after the first AGM

We fold these into an annual engagement so nothing depends on a founder remembering a form. See ROC and annual compliance for the full calendar.

LLP registration

An LLP is registered through the FiLLiP form after name reservation, and the LLP agreement must be filed in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. It suits professional practices, family businesses and joint ventures that want limited liability without the meeting and audit machinery of a company. Note that an LLP still needs its accounts audited once turnover or contribution crosses the prescribed limits, and files Form 11 and Form 8 annually.

Startup India (DPIIT) recognition

If your entity is a private limited company, LLP or registered partnership within the prescribed age and turnover limits and is working on innovation or a scalable business model, DPIIT recognition is worth obtaining. It opens access to government schemes, self-certification under certain labour and environmental laws, and eligibility to apply for the income tax exemption available to eligible startups. We prepare the application and the supporting write-up.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to register a private limited company in Chandigarh?

The total is made up of three separate things: government filing fees and stamp duty (which vary with the state of the registered office and the authorised capital), the cost of digital signature certificates for each director, and professional fees. We give a single all-inclusive figure before starting and show you which part is a government charge, so there are no additions later.

How long does company registration take?

With complete and consistent documents, name approval typically takes one to three working days and incorporation about a week thereafter, subject to the Registrar's queries. Delays almost always come from mismatched address proof or a proposed name that conflicts with an existing company or trademark — both of which we check before filing.

Can I register a company at my home address in Chandigarh?

Yes. A residential address can serve as the registered office provided you can produce a recent utility bill for the premises and a no-objection certificate from the owner. The registered office is where official correspondence is served, so it must be an address where you can reliably receive post.

Do I need GST registration immediately after incorporation?

Not automatically. GST registration becomes mandatory when you cross the turnover threshold, make inter-state supplies of goods, sell through an e-commerce operator, or fall into one of the other categories requiring compulsory registration. Registering before you need to means filing returns every month from that point, including nil returns, so it is worth deciding deliberately rather than by default.

Should I choose an LLP or a private limited company?

If you intend to raise external investment, a private limited company is effectively the only practical option — investors subscribe to shares, and LLPs cannot offer them. If you are a professional practice or a closely held family business with no plans to bring in outside capital, an LLP gives you limited liability with a lighter and cheaper annual compliance load.

Can NRIs or foreign nationals be directors in an Indian company?

Yes, subject to the requirement that at least one director is resident in India, and to FEMA rules on foreign investment including the reporting that follows an infusion of foreign funds. Documents of foreign nationals need notarisation or apostille. We handle the incorporation and the subsequent FDI reporting together.
