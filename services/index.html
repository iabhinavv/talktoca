Frequently asked questions

Can I engage you for a single filing rather than an annual retainer?

Yes. One-off work is common — a single income tax return, a company incorporation, a GST registration or a reply to a notice. We quote it as a standalone job. Most clients who start with one-off work move to an annual engagement once they see how much of the calendar they were carrying themselves.

How quickly can you start?

For registrations and one-off filings, usually within a day or two of receiving documents. For a full handover from another accountant we ask for a week to reconcile the portals and tell you where things actually stand before we take responsibility for them.

Do you work with clients outside Chandigarh?

Regularly. Filings, registrations and audits are handled through government portals and digital signatures, so physical location rarely matters. We have clients across Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Delhi NCR and further afield who work with us entirely remotely.

What documents will you need from me to begin?

It depends on the service, and each service page lists its own requirements. As a baseline we ask for PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor, partners or directors, proof of business address, bank statements for the period, and access to any existing GST, income tax or MCA logins.
