GST registration limit in India: when you must register

Turnover is only one of the triggers. Several situations require GST registration from the first rupee — including one that catches out almost every tricity business.

"Do I need to register for GST?" is the single most common question we are asked by new businesses, and the answer is more layered than the headline threshold suggests. Turnover is only one of the triggers — several situations require registration from the first rupee.

The turnover thresholds

For businesses in normal category states, registration becomes compulsory once aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds broadly ₹40 lakh for suppliers of goods and ₹20 lakh for suppliers of services. Special category states apply lower limits. Chandigarh is a normal category Union Territory.

Two details in that sentence do most of the damage:

  • "Aggregate turnover" is computed on the PAN, across India. If you run two businesses under the same PAN, their turnovers are added together. Exempt supplies, exports and inter-state supplies all count towards the aggregate, even though they may not attract tax themselves.
  • Goods and services are treated differently. A business supplying both is generally tested against the lower services limit — a point that catches out businesses that think of themselves as traders but bill a service component.

When registration is compulsory regardless of turnover

The threshold is irrelevant if any of these apply:

  • You make inter-state taxable supplies of goods. One consignment across a state border can trigger registration.
  • You sell through an e-commerce operator that is required to collect tax at source.
  • You are liable to pay tax under reverse charge.
  • You are a casual taxable person — for example exhibiting at a trade fair in another state — or a non-resident taxable person.
  • You act as an agent supplying on behalf of another, or as an input service distributor.

The tricity version of this problem. Chandigarh's GST state code is 04, Punjab's is 03, Haryana's is 06. A business in Sector 8C supplying goods to a customer in Mohali or Panchkula is making an inter-state supply — IGST, not CGST plus SGST — and if it holds stock across that border it may need a second registration. The distance is twenty minutes; the compliance position is entirely different.

Should you register voluntarily?

Voluntary registration below the threshold is allowed, and sometimes sensible. It makes sense if your customers are businesses who want to claim credit on your invoices, if you buy substantial inputs on which you would otherwise absorb the tax, or if you are about to cross the threshold anyway.

It is a poor idea if you sell mainly to end consumers and have few input costs. Registration is a permanent monthly obligation: returns are due whether or not you traded, and late fees apply to nil returns exactly as they do to any other. Businesses register in optimism and then pay late fees for years on a dormant GSTIN.

What happens if you cross the threshold and do not register

Liability does not wait for registration. Tax becomes payable from the date you were required to be registered, with interest, and penalties apply for the failure. You also cannot claim input tax credit for the unregistered period against that liability, so the cost lands entirely on your margin. Monitoring turnover as it approaches the limit is far cheaper than reconstructing the position later.

Documents you will need

  • PAN of the business and of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Aadhaar of the authorised signatory, for e-KYC authentication
  • Certificate of incorporation or partnership deed, as applicable
  • Proof of the principal place of business — utility bill, rent agreement, owner's NOC
  • Bank details: cancelled cheque or first page of a statement
  • Photographs and the authorisation for the signatory

Completing Aadhaar authentication materially speeds up approval. Without it, applications usually go to physical verification of the premises.

After registration: what you have signed up for

Registration is the start of a monthly rhythm — GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, or the quarterly QRMP route if you are eligible, plus the annual return where applicable. Input tax credit has to be reconciled against GSTR-2B every month, because credit that does not appear there is not available to you no matter how genuine the invoice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GST registration limit for services in India?

Broadly ₹20 lakh of aggregate turnover in normal category states, with a lower limit in special category states. This is half the limit that applies to suppliers of goods, and a business supplying both goods and services is generally tested against the lower services limit.

Is GST registration compulsory for online sellers?

If you sell through an e-commerce operator that is required to collect tax at source, registration is compulsory regardless of turnover. If you sell through your own website directly to customers, the ordinary threshold rules apply — but the moment you ship goods across a state border, the inter-state supply trigger comes into play.

Can I cancel a GST registration I no longer need?

Yes. Apply for cancellation through the portal, file the final return, and settle any liability including reversal of credit on stock held. Simply stopping filing is not cancellation — late fees keep accruing on an active GSTIN, and the registration can be cancelled by the department in a way that causes problems if you later want to register again.

How long does GST registration take in Chandigarh?

Where Aadhaar authentication is completed, registration is typically granted within a few working days. Where it is not completed, the application usually goes for physical verification of the premises, which extends the timeline. Unclear address proof is the most common reason for a query.
