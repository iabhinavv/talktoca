Received a GST notice? How to read it and reply

Most GST notices start as a mismatch. A step-by-step response, and the appeal route now that the GST Appellate Tribunal is operational.

Most GST notices are raised automatically when returns do not match — GSTR-1 against GSTR-3B, or input tax credit claimed against GSTR-2B. A prompt, documented reply usually closes them; ignoring one is what turns a mismatch into a demand.

First, identify the notice

Common GST notices and usual response periods
FormWhat it meansUsual time to reply
REG-03Clarification sought on a registration application7 working days
REG-17Show cause notice for cancellation of registration7 working days
ASMT-10Discrepancies found in scrutiny of returns30 days (reply in ASMT-11)
DRC-01BLiability in GSTR-1 higher than paid in GSTR-3B7 days
DRC-01CITC in GSTR-3B higher than available in GSTR-2B7 days
DRC-01APre-show-cause intimation of tax ascertainedAs stated
DRC-01Show cause notice (demand)As stated, usually 30 days

Always go by the time limit printed on your own notice.

Step-by-step response

  1. Check authenticity. Genuine notices appear on gst.gov.in under Services → User Services → View Notices and Orders. Do not pay through links in emails.
  2. Note the period, section and amount. Demands for FY 2024-25 onwards are issued under section 74A; earlier years still use section 73 (non-fraud) or 74 (fraud or suppression).
  3. Reconcile GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B and your books. Most differences come from timing, later-month amendments, supplier non-filing or credit notes.
  4. Pay what is due, dispute what is not. Paying genuine short tax with interest through DRC-03 before a show cause notice limits penalties.
  5. Reply on the portal with a reconciliation, invoices, ledgers and legal grounds, and ask for a personal hearing if a demand is proposed.
  6. Keep the acknowledgement and track the order.

If an adverse order is passed

GST appeal route
LevelForumTime limitPre-deposit on disputed tax
First appealAppellate Authority3 months from the order10%, plus the admitted amount
Second appealGST Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT)3 months from the first appellate orderA further 10%

Caps apply to pre-deposits. GSTAT is now operational. For orders communicated before 1 April 2026 the backlog window ran to 30 June 2026; later filings need a condonation of delay application. For orders from 1 April 2026 the normal 3-month limit applies.

Avoiding notices

  • Reconcile GSTR-2B with the purchase register every month before filing GSTR-3B.
  • Follow up with suppliers who have not filed GSTR-1.
  • File GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C carefully — many scrutiny notices start there.
  • Keep registration details (address, partners, bank) up to date.

Frequently asked questions

Will a GST notice lead to cancellation of my registration?

Only REG-17 relates to cancellation. Answer it within the time allowed to avoid suspension.

Can I reply after the deadline?

The portal may still accept a reply, but the officer can proceed without it. Request an extension in writing before the deadline if you need time.
