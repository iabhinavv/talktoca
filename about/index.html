About CA Anil K. Verma & Associates

A Chandigarh Chartered Accountancy firm advising Indian businesses on tax, audit and compliance.

A practice built around answering, not deflecting

CA Anil K. Verma & Associates is a Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (FRN 019748N), led by CA Anil K. Verma (ICAI Membership No. 097020). The firm is based on Madhya Marg in Sector 8C, Chandigarh. We advise startups, SMEs, professionals and established companies on taxation, audit, incorporation and corporate compliance — across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and, increasingly, wherever in India our clients happen to be.

The firm was built on a straightforward observation: most business owners do not need more information about tax law. They need someone who will read their specific situation, say what it means in plain language, and then take responsibility for the filing. That is the standard we hold ourselves to on every engagement, whether it is a first-time proprietor's return or a group audit.

Our team brings together Chartered Accountants, tax practitioners and compliance staff so that a client's GST position, income tax exposure and corporate filings are looked at as one picture rather than three separate jobs handed to three separate people.

How we work

Four principles we work to

We tell you the downside

If a position is aggressive, if a past filing is wrong, or if a plan will not survive scrutiny, you hear it from us before you commit — not after a notice arrives.

We own the calendar

Deadlines are tracked on our side. You get a document request ahead of time and a confirmation once the return is filed, with the acknowledgement on record.

Terms are in writing

Scope, deliverables and professional fees are set out in an engagement letter at the start, with statutory and government charges itemised separately.

One person knows your file

A named relationship manager backed by a qualified CA. You do not re-explain your business every time you call, and nothing falls between desks.

Core values

What the firm is built on

Integrity

Independence and ethical standards are not negotiable in this profession. We decline work where we cannot act independently, and we say so.

Rigour

Accurate, timely and evidenced work. Every position we take is one we are prepared to defend in front of the department.

Clarity

Advice in language a business owner can act on, with the reasoning shown rather than hidden behind section numbers.

Disciplines under one roof

Businesses rarely have a problem that is purely a tax problem. A funding round is a valuation question, a company law question and a tax question at the same time. A departmental notice about input credit is a GST question that begins in the purchase ledger. We staff engagements so those threads are handled together:

  • Direct taxation — corporate and personal income tax, TDS, advance tax, capital gains, assessments and appeals.
  • Indirect taxation — GST registration, returns, input credit reconciliation, refunds, audits and departmental representation.
  • Audit and assurance — statutory, tax, internal, stock and management audits.
  • Corporate law and secretarial — incorporation, ROC filings, registers, minutes, charges and event-based compliance.
  • Advisory — virtual CFO, business valuation, due diligence, restructuring and funding support.

If you are weighing up whether to move your work to us, the fastest way to judge the fit is a short call about a live problem. Get in touch — there is no charge for the first conversation.
