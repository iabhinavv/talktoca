Why tricity businesses need multi-state GST advice

Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula operate as a single economy across three separate GST jurisdictions. Chandigarh carries state code 04, Punjab 03 and Haryana 06. A firm with its office in Sector 8C and a warehouse in Zirakpur is dealing with two registrations, and a supply between them is an inter-state supply attracting IGST rather than CGST and SGST.

This trips up new businesses constantly. It affects where you need to register, which head of tax you charge, how e-way bills are generated for movements that take twenty minutes, and how input credit flows between locations. It is one of the more common reasons tricity businesses end up with a mismatch notice, and it is entirely avoidable with the structure set up correctly at the start.

Working across the tricity? Before you take a second premises in another state, get the GST structure reviewed. Restructuring registrations after the fact is far more painful than planning them. Call +91 94171 21061.

Choosing a CA in Chandigarh: what to actually check

Fee quotes are the least useful basis for comparison, because they rarely cover the same scope. More telling questions:

  • Who signs and who does the work? Ask whether a qualified CA reviews your file or only signs at the end.
  • What is inside the annual fee? Nail down whether GST returns, TDS returns, ROC filings, notices and audit are included or billed separately.
  • How are deadlines tracked? A practice that relies on you to remember is one that will cost you late fees.
  • What happens when a notice arrives? Response drafting and representation are the moments the relationship is actually tested.
  • Will they tell you when you are wrong? The value of the professional is in the position they refuse to sign, not the one they will.

We are happy to be asked all five. If the answers suit you, the next step is a short call about your live situation.

Frequently asked questions

Where is your CA office in Chandigarh?

Our office is at SCO 118-19, 2nd Floor, Madhya Marg, Sector 8C, Chandigarh. Madhya Marg is the main arterial road through the city, so the office is straightforward to reach from Sector 17, the Industrial Area, Mohali and Panchkula. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

What is the GST state code for Chandigarh?

Chandigarh's GST state code is 04, so every GSTIN registered in the Union Territory begins with those digits. Neighbouring codes matter for tricity businesses: Punjab is 03 and Haryana is 06. A supply from Chandigarh to Mohali is therefore an inter-state supply attracting IGST, even though the two are a short drive apart — a point that catches out a lot of new businesses operating across the tricity.

Which Registrar of Companies covers Chandigarh?

Companies incorporated in Chandigarh and Punjab fall under the Registrar of Companies, Chandigarh. Incorporation and annual filings are made through the MCA portal, so the process is the same wherever you sit, but jurisdiction matters when there is correspondence, adjudication or a physical hearing.

Do you serve Mohali, Panchkula and Zirakpur as well?

Yes. The tricity functions as one economy even though it spans three GST jurisdictions, and a large share of our work is for businesses in Mohali's IT City and industrial phases, Panchkula's industrial area, and Zirakpur. We handle the multi-state GST positions that come with operating across that boundary.

Can you handle a scrutiny notice or GST audit locally?

Yes. We draft and file replies, handle e-proceedings, and appear before the income tax and GST authorities where representation is required. Being in Chandigarh means we can attend in person when a hearing calls for it rather than relying entirely on written submissions.
