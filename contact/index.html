Getting here

The office is on Madhya Marg in Sector 8C, the main arterial road running east to west through Chandigarh. Coming from Sector 17 or the ISBT, head north along Madhya Marg; from the Mohali side, Madhya Marg is reached via the Sector 8/9 stretch. Parking is available in the SCO block.

What to bring to a first meeting

Nothing is mandatory for an initial conversation. If you want the meeting to be productive rather than exploratory, bringing the following helps us give you specifics instead of generalities:

  • PAN of the business and of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Last filed income tax return and financial statements, if any
  • GST registration certificate and the last three months of returns, if registered
  • Any notice or communication you have received from the department
  • Bank statements for the current financial year

Office hours

Mon – Sat, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays. During peak filing periods — the weeks before the income tax and GST annual deadlines — we recommend calling ahead to fix a time rather than dropping in.
